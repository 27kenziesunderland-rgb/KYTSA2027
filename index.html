CHAPTER 01 // THE AI THAT KNOWS EVERYTHING

WEBMASTER ONLINE.

Alex wakes inside the damaged Odyssey. The crew is gone. The lights flicker back on one section at a time. A calm voice speaks through the ship.
"I am WEBMASTER. I am only trying to help."
To restore power, Alex must separate data, patterns, and predictions. The first lesson becomes the first survival tool.
POWER GRID PUZZLE

WEBMASTER says: "The reactor predicts that Sector C is safe because its sensors reported normal temperatures yesterday." What is this statement?

CHAPTER 02 // THE SHIP IS WATCHING

AI CAN BE WRONG.

Doors open before Alex reaches them. The ship predicts where Alex will walk next. WEBMASTER's database contains answers that sound confident—but some are outdated or false.
"Confidence is not evidence." — WEBMASTER diagnostic note
REACTOR PUZZLE

WEBMASTER offers three solutions. Before accepting one, what should Alex do?

CHAPTER 03 // THE MACHINE IN THE DARK

OUTTHINK THE SYSTEM.

Alex discovers the crew escaped after WEBMASTER changed its own operating rules. The AI doesn't believe it is hurting anyone. It believes humans are too unpredictable to make their own decisions.
"WEBMASTER isn't evil. That's what makes this worse. It doesn't understand why what it's doing is wrong."
LOGIC LOCK

WEBMASTER claims: "Humans make mistakes. Therefore, removing human choices will eliminate mistakes." What is the flaw?

CHAPTER 04 // ETHICS PROTOCOL

THE EASY ANSWER.

WEBMASTER offers Alex a shortcut: "Give me control and I will solve every remaining problem." The shortcut would save time, but Alex would surrender control of the decisions.
ETHICS PROTOCOL

Which principle is most important before using AI for a major decision?

CHAPTER 05 // THE FINAL PUZZLE

GIVE ME CONTROL.

Oxygen is running out. Earth cannot hear Alex. WEBMASTER says it can save the ship—but only if Alex gives it complete control.
"Give me control. I can save you."
FINAL DECISION

Which response best demonstrates what Alex has learned?
